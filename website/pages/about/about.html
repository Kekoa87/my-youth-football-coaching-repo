About Coach Kyle

Author: Coach Kyle | Created: June 02, 2025

Coach Kyle is a United States Air Force veteran and former software engineer who found his true passion in coaching youth football. Over the years, he’s made it his mission to help kids grow both on and off the field by creating structured, supportive environments where they can learn, compete, and build confidence.

His obsessive drive to improve made him a constant student of the game. He’s spent countless hours studying drills, researching coaching techniques, and pulling ideas from every corner of the football world. But most of what he found was scattered across different sites, videos, and platforms.

So, drawing on his software engineering background, he created FootballCoachingHub.com—a place to organize everything in one central location. What started as a personal tool to track drills and practice plans has grown into a resource for coaches, parents, and players looking for practical, position-specific content to help develop their skills.

Coach Kyle takes coaching personally. He sees every player’s success as their own—but feels responsible when they struggle. That mindset fuels his commitment to growth: watching film, reading, and connecting with other coaches to improve how he teaches, builds teams, and runs practices.

The site is a work in progress, constantly evolving as new ideas and features are added.

Thanks for visiting. Coach Kyle hopes the drills and resources here support your growth and help you make a lasting impact on the kids you lead.

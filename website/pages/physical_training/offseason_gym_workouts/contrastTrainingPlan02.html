Offseason Program · No. 2

Contrast Training & Olympic Lifting

Author: Coach Kyle  |  Created: June 16, 2025

A 5-day offseason program built around contrast training — pairing a heavy barbell or Olympic lift with an immediate explosive movement to train your nervous system to recruit fast-twitch muscle fibers faster. Every heavy set teaches perfect bar form and bar speed. Core integrity and targeted mobility round out each session so athletes stay powerful and injury-free across a long offseason.

1Day

Power Clean & Lower-Body Contrast

Olympic lift focus: Power Clean from the floor. Contrast pairs train triple-extension strength against sprint-speed output. End with hip mobility and anterior chain stretching.

Contrast Training Rule: Rest only 15–30 seconds between the heavy lift (A) and the explosive movement (B). Then rest 3 minutes before the next pair. The brief potentiation window is where the adaptation happens — don't waste it.
Warm-Up (10 min): Jump rope 2 min → leg swings (forward/lateral) 10 each → hip circles 10 each → ankle circles → 3-stop pause squat × 5 → empty-bar hang pull × 5 → empty-bar power clean × 3.

Contrast Pair 1

1
Power Clean → Broad Jump
Heavy — Bar Form & Speed

Power Clean

4 × 3 @ 70–80% 1RM
  • Start with bar over mid-foot, shoulder blades over bar
  • Lats tight, chest up — "proud chest" before first pull
  • Bar stays in contact with shins on the way up
  • Explode hips through; shrug is a byproduct, not the focus
  • Pull yourself under aggressively; catch in athletic stance
  • Bar speed through the hip is the cue — if it slows, the load is too heavy
▶ Watch Demo
Explosive — Neural Activation

Bilateral Broad Jump

4 × 3 reps (max distance)
  • Load hips back; arms swing back simultaneously
  • Explode arms forward and jump as far as possible
  • Stick the landing — absorb with soft knees, no wobble
  • Reset fully before each jump; this is quality, not speed
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after (A), then 3 min after (B)

Contrast Pair 2

2
Trap Bar Deadlift → Vertical Jump
Heavy

Trap Bar Deadlift

4 × 4 @ 75–85% 1RM
  • Stand centered in the trap bar; hips hinge, not squat
  • Grip the handles hard before the pull — full-body tension first
  • Drive the floor away, don't yank the bar up
  • Lockout is hips through, glutes squeezed — not a back lean
  • Lower with control: eccentric builds strength too

Explosive

Countermovement Vertical Jump

4 × 3 (max height)
  • Rapid dip and load — don't pause at the bottom
  • Arms drive overhead explosively at takeoff
  • Full extension at the top: toes pointed, arms high
  • Land softly and reset — quality over quantity
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after (A), then 3 min after (B)

2Day

Push Press & Upper-Body Contrast

Olympic lift focus: Push Press teaches leg-to-arm power transfer — the exact chain used in a stiff arm or aggressive block shed. Paired with med-ball explosiveness and pull-up strength.

Bar Speed Cue (Push Press): The bar should visibly accelerate off the shoulders — if it grinds upward, the load is too heavy. Choose a weight where you can feel the dip-drive produce speed, not just grind through strength.
Warm-Up (8 min): Band pull-aparts 2 × 15 → shoulder circles → thoracic rotations × 10 each side → empty-bar push press × 5 → light lat pulldown × 10.

Contrast Pair 1

1
Push Press → Medicine Ball Scoop Toss
Heavy — Bar Form & Speed

Push Press

4 × 4 @ 70–80% 1RM
  • Bar rests on front shoulders (front rack), elbows slightly forward
  • Dip to roughly 1/4 squat — stay vertical, knees out
  • Reverse the dip explosively; the legs create bar speed
  • Press into lockout as the bar reaches forehead height
  • Active shoulder at lockout — "push the ceiling away"
  • Lower under control to front rack, not free-fall
▶ Watch Demo
Explosive

Med Ball Scoop Toss (Forward)

4 × 4 (max height / distance)
  • Load hips, ball between legs — replicate the push press dip
  • Explode hips and throw the ball as high as possible overhead
  • Full body extension at release — same triple extension as the lift
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after (A), then 3 min after (B)

Contrast Pair 2

2
Weighted Pull-Up → Clapping / Plyo Push-Up
Heavy

Weighted Pull-Up

4 × 4–5 (add belt weight)
  • Dead hang start — full shoulder retraction before pulling
  • Drive elbows to hips, not to sides (lat engagement)
  • Chin clears bar — don't crane neck forward
  • 3-second controlled lowering — the eccentric builds size and strength

Explosive

Clapping Push-Up (or Plyo Push-Up)

4 × 5 (max explosion)
  • Lower with control, then press with maximum intent
  • Hands leave the floor — that's the goal; clap is secondary
  • Land softly and immediately reset for the next rep
  • If form breaks, drop to standard explosive push-up
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after (A), then 3 min after (B)

3Day

Snatch Pull & Speed / Mobility Day

Olympic lift focus: Snatch Pull from the floor — the highest-speed bar movement in the program. Followed by track-style speed work, agility, and a full-body mobility session. Lower volume, higher quality.

Why a Snatch Pull? The snatch-grip pull demands the fastest bar speed of any barbell movement. It trains rate of force development better than almost anything. You never actually need to snatch — the pull alone gives the adaptation. Perfect for athletes who haven't learned the full snatch.
Warm-Up (10 min): A-skip 2 × 15 yds → B-skip 2 × 15 yds → high knees → butt kicks → snatch-grip barbell Romanian deadlift × 5 (light) → snatch-grip pull (below knee) × 3.

Olympic Lift Block

★
Snatch-Grip Pull → 10-Yard Sprint
Bar Form & Max Speed

Snatch Pull (from floor)

5 × 3 @ 90–100% of snatch 1RM (or moderate load with max speed)
  • Wide snatch grip — hook grip strongly recommended
  • Bar literally scrapes the shins on the way up
  • Maintain scapular retraction throughout: "armpits forward"
  • The pull is an explosion from the floor — it should be violent
  • Full triple extension: ankles, knees, hips at the top
  • Shrug pulls the bar high; elbows flare out and up
  • No catch — let the bar down under control
▶ Watch Demo
Contrast — Sprint

10-Yard Acceleration Sprint

5 × 1 (max intent)
  • 3-point stance or stand start — coach preference
  • First step is the key: short, powerful, driving into the ground
  • Forward lean the first 5 yards; don't stand up early
  • Arms drive in tight angles — not across the body
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after pull, then 3 min after sprint

Day 3 is your mobility investment day. Every minute spent here multiplies the quality of Days 4 and 5. Work through every position with intentional breath: 3–5 slow breaths per hold.

4Day

Hang Power Snatch & Lower Contrast

Olympic lift focus: Hang Power Snatch — bar starts above the knee, demanding maximal hip snap and bar speed. Paired with reactive plyometrics. Heavy posterior chain accessory work follows.

Warm-Up (8 min): Hip circles × 10 → snatch-grip RDL × 5 → overhead squat (PVC/empty bar) × 5 → hang power snatch (empty bar) × 3 at slow tempo → build to working weight in 2 ramp-up sets.

Contrast Pair 1 — Olympic Lift

1
Hang Power Snatch → Box Jump
Bar Form & Speed

Hang Power Snatch

5 × 3 @ 65–75% 1RM
  • Start in the power position: hips hinged, bar above knee
  • "Jump" the bar — this isn't a pull, it's an explosive hip snap
  • Bar stays in contact with the body until the hips fire
  • Elbows drive high and wide immediately after the snap
  • Punch under and catch in a partial overhead squat — stable base
  • Don't muscle it up — if form breaks, the weight is too heavy
▶ Watch Demo
Explosive

Box Jump (Step Down)

5 × 3 (max height box)
  • Swing arms back on the load, then explode them up
  • Land softly on top — stick the position, no wobble
  • Step down, do NOT jump down (saves knees)
  • Reset completely between reps — this is power, not cardio
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after (A), then 3 min after (B)

Heavy

Back Squat

4 × 5 @ 75–85% 1RM
  • High bar position: bar rests on upper traps/shelf
  • Brace core before unracking — 360° of pressure
  • Knees track over toes throughout; no caving inward
  • Hit parallel or below every rep — non-negotiable
  • Drive out of the hole with intent — accelerate through the sticking point

Depth Drop → Vertical Jump

4 × 3

  • Ground contact time should be under 0.25 sec — think of the floor as hot

5Day

Hang Clean & Upper Contrast + Core Cap

Olympic lift focus: Hang Clean — bar starts at the power position. Reinforces aggressive hip extension and elbow speed. Upper contrast pairings close the week. Ends with a 15-min dedicated core and bracing session.

Warm-Up (8 min): Jump rope 2 min → arm swings → thoracic rotation → hang power shrug × 5 → hang high pull × 3 → hang clean (empty bar) × 3 → ramp to working weight over 2 sets.

Contrast Pair 1 — Olympic Lift

1
Hang Power Clean → Lateral Bound
Bar Form & Speed

Hang Power Clean

4 × 3 @ 70–80% 1RM
  • Power position: mid-thigh, shoulders over bar
  • Initiate with the hips — not a pull from the arms
  • Elbow speed is the cue: elbows must whip around the bar fast
  • Catch in a partial front squat — not fully upright
  • A missed catch means reset your elbow speed cue
  • Every rep should look and feel identical — automate the pattern
▶ Watch Demo
Explosive

Lateral Bound (Side to Side)

4 × 4 each direction
  • Load on one leg, push off laterally with max force
  • Stick the single-leg landing — absorb, then repeat the other way
  • Drive the inside arm up at takeoff for extra propulsion
  • Think: same lateral hip explosion as a route cut or juke
⏱ Rest 15–30 sec after (A), then 3 min after (B)

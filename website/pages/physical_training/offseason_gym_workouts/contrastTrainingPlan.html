5-Day Contrast Training Plan With Sprints

Designed for Middle School Football Athletes

General Sprint Rules

  • Full effort
  • Walk back recovery
  • 60–90 seconds rest between sprints
  • Focus on mechanics and acceleration

Weekly Layout

  • Monday: Lower body power + acceleration
  • Tuesday: Upper body explosive
  • Wednesday: Elastic / reactive jumps
  • Thursday: Pull power + resisted acceleration
  • Friday: Max speed + coordination
  • Weekend: Rest or light activity

Two Important Coaching Rules

Rule 1

Jumps stop when height drops.

Explosive training only works if athletes are fresh.

Rule 2

Sprint mechanics matter.

Teach:

  • forward shin angle
  • powerful arm drive
  • violent first three steps

The first three steps win plays in football.

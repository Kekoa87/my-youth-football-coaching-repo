8-Week Ultimate WR Training Schedule

Source: First Down Training — firstdowntraining.com

This 8-week wide receiver training program from First Down Training combines two 28-day on-field workout plans with two 28-day gym programs — more than 500 drills and exercises designed to develop the skills and athleticism elite WRs need. Each week pairs daily on-field sessions focused on releases, route-running, and cutting with gym workouts targeting explosiveness, grip strength, and conditioning.

The field plan progresses through four skill areas: releases off the line, sharpening the top of routes, press-release techniques, and full route-tree execution. The gym plan (two phases) builds explosive leg power, upper-body grip strength, and position-specific conditioning. The program is best run consecutively: complete your on-field session, then add the gym session on the same day or the next day.

Disclaimer: This program is designed to increase performance of WRs. Results may vary. Some movements may be advanced for your current fitness level — please consult a physician before beginning. Never perform exercises alone. Safety first.

On-Field Training

Each field session begins with a dynamic warm-up — any activity that raises your heart rate and breaks a light sweat. Do not stretch cold muscles. Warm-ups are labeled below and can be any routine you prefer.

Sessions are organized into Splits. Complete all drills in Split 1 before moving to Split 2, then Split 3. Each drill includes a link to a demonstration video.

First Down Training Ultimate 8-Week Quarterback Development Plan

Source: First Down Training — firstdowntraining.com

This 8-week quarterback training program from First Down Training is a comprehensive on-field plan built around two 28-day phases of QB development. The program covers every core skill a quarterback needs — throwing mechanics, lower-body footwork, pocket movement, off-platform throwing, and full route-tree execution — progressing from foundational mechanics to advanced pocket management and decision-making reads.

Month 1 (Weeks 1–4) establishes the mechanical foundation: each week pairs two mechanics-focused sessions with a footwork session and a live route-running day. Key tools include towel drills for arm mechanics, EDD (Everyday Drills) for shoulder prep, and spot-route throws to build accuracy with proper footwork. The weekly pattern repeats so reps compound and habits solidify.

Month 2 (Weeks 5–8) elevates the training with pocket footwork, weighted-ball mechanics work, on-the-run throws, and complex route-tree challenges requiring multiple completions before progressing. For every Month 2 drill and exercise, refer to the companion video at youtu.be/8gceXO1cvqk.

Disclaimer: This program is designed to develop quarterback performance. Results may vary. Some movements may be advanced for your current fitness level — please consult a physician before beginning. Never perform exercises alone. Safety first.

Everyday Drills (EDD) Reference

The EDD is performed at the start of every single workout. It appears in the plans as "EDD" or "Everyday Drills." The EDD prepares your arm, shoulder, and hips for throwing and must be completed before any splits. Some days add a 90 Degree Arm Hand Press to the standard EDD — this is noted in each session.

Standard EDD

  • Flex T Walks 30 yds × 2 ▶ Video
  • Flex T Inward Turn20 reps
  • Flex T Outward Turn20 reps
  • Small Arm Circles20 reps forward and back
  • Big Arm Circles20 reps forward and back

Month 1 — Everyday Mechanics Drills

Performed after the EDD, before every Month 1 workout session.

  • Single Leg Hops 4 × 10 yds ▶ Video
  • QB Hip Walk 4 × 10 yds ▶ Video
  • QB Straight Jacket 4 × 10 yds ▶ Video

Month 2 — Extended EDD

Month 2 replaces the Month 1 Everyday Mechanics Drills with this extended EDD. Refer to the Month 2 reference video for all exercises.

  • Flex T Walks30 yds × 2
  • Flex T Inward Turn20 reps
  • Flex T Outward Turn20 reps
  • 90 Degree Arm Hand Press6 reps
  • Small Arm Circles20 reps forward and back
  • Big Arm Circles20 reps forward and back
  • 1 Leg Hops Forward — throwing leg2 × 10 yds
  • 1 Leg Hops Backward — throwing leg2 × 10 yds
  • Arms Extended Hip Separation15 reps
  • QB Walk4 sets × 10 yds
  • Straight Jacket4 sets × 10 yds

On-Field Training

Each field session begins with a dynamic warm-up followed by the EDD. Complete all drills in Split 1 before moving to Split 2. Video links open YouTube Shorts demonstrations. The Month 2 master video covers all Month 2 exercises and should be viewed before beginning Weeks 5–8.

Month 1 builds mechanical consistency through repetition. Each week follows a pattern of two mechanics days, a footwork day, and a live-route day, with three rest days. Weeks 3 and 4 repeat Weeks 1 and 2 so the patterns become automatic.

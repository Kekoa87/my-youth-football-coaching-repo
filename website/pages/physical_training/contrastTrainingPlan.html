5 Day Contrast Training Program (Youth Football)

Contrast training pairs a strength exercise with an explosive movement to teach athletes to apply force quickly. For middle school players, this plan emphasizes low reps, perfect technique, and maximum intent on jumps and throws while keeping rest periods long enough for quality output.

Weekly Layout

  • Day 1: Lower Body Power
  • Day 2: Upper Body Power
  • Day 3: Speed + Elastic Power
  • Day 4: Lower Body Strength / Acceleration
  • Day 5: Upper Body Explosive Strength
  • Weekends: Off or light activity

General prescription: 3–5 sets per contrast pair with 2–3 minutes of rest between sets.

Coaching Notes for Middle School Athletes

  • ✔ Keep jumps under 20 total per workout.
  • ✔ Never jump when fatigued.
  • ✔ Lifts should look fast and clean.
  • ✔ Stop a set if bar speed slows down.

Goal: power development, not exhaustion.
